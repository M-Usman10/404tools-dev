Privacy Policy

Profile Switcher for Claude · Last updated 11 November 2026

Profile Switcher for Claude ("the extension") is an independent, unofficial browser extension that lets you save your own claude.ai and chatgpt.com logins, use a different one in each tab, and optionally sync them between your own browsers. This policy explains exactly what it does with your data.

The short version

  • Without sync, everything stays in your own browser.
  • If you choose Sign in to sync, your saved profiles are sent over HTTPS to the extension's own sync service (Microsoft Azure), stored encrypted, and used only to give the same profiles to your other browsers.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, analyzed or used for advertising.

What data is handled

  • Session cookies for claude.ai and anthropic.com, and for chatgpt.com and auth.openai.com (including httpOnly authentication cookies). These are what keep you logged in.
  • The names you give your profiles.
  • The email address of each Claude or ChatGPT account, read from that site when you save or open a profile, so the extension can tell accounts apart, avoid duplicates and show when a login was signed out.
  • If you turn on sync: your Microsoft account email and an anonymous account identifier from Microsoft sign-in, used only to know which synced data is yours.

The extension does not read your chats, prompts, files or any page content.

Where the data is stored

  • On your device: in the browser's extension storage (chrome.storage).
  • In the cloud (only if you sign in to sync): in a private Microsoft Azure storage account (Central India region) operated by the developer. Each user's data is one file, encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it is written, and only reachable through the sync service after Microsoft sign-in.

How the data is used

Solely to:

  • send each tab the login of the profile you opened in it,
  • keep saved logins current as claude.ai refreshes them,
  • keep the same profiles in sync across your own signed-in browsers.

Who it is shared with

No one. Data is processed by Microsoft Azure only as the hosting provider. Microsoft sign-in is handled by Microsoft under its own privacy policy.

Security note

Saved logins give access to the claude.ai accounts they belong to. Profiles on your device are not encrypted by the extension, so only use it on a device you trust. Anyone who can sign in to your sync account can download your synced profiles to their browser.

Removing your data

  • Deleting a profile removes it from this device and, with sync on, from your other devices and the cloud copy.
  • Sign out in the extension and choose to delete your synced data to remove everything stored in the cloud for your account.
  • Removing the extension deletes all data stored on that device.
  • You can also email the address below to have your cloud data deleted.

Permissions

cookies, storage, tabs, scripting, declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess, webRequest, webNavigation, identity, alarms, and host access limited to claude.ai, *.anthropic.com, chatgpt.com, auth.openai.com and the extension's own sync service. See the store listing for what each one is used for.
